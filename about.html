About the Founder

Nathan PiPs, popularly known as Nathan’s PiPs or Sovereign, is a highly successful financial trader, philanthropist, and founder of the Nathan Pips Charity Foundation. He is also a best-selling author of The Roadmap I.O. and The Game of Finance, as well as a global educator with over seven years of experience in the financial markets. Known for his innovative strategies and deep understanding of global economics, he has built a reputation as one of the top traders of his generation. Nathan PiPs has authored several widely acclaimed books, including Psychology of the Market Wizards and The Roadmap I.O., which have become essential reading for traders worldwide. He has mentored thousands of students across the globe through online courses and one-on-one coaching, helping them develop the skills and mindset needed for long-term trading success and sustainability. Born and raised in the Sub-Saharan region of Africa, his travels over the years have provided him with unique insights into international markets, further enriching his teachings. Today, the name Nathan PiPs and the brand Decode Foreign Exchange Academy continue to inspire and educate aspiring traders, leaving a lasting impact on the global financial community.

Founder & Lead Analyst

Nathan Pips

🏛 A Chattered Financial Analyst CFA & a GARP certified Financial Risk Manager

My journey into forex began not in a trading room, but in frustration. After years of consuming free content, blowing multiple accounts, and questioning whether consistent profitability was even real. I committed to understanding the market the way institutional players do.

The turning point came when I discovered that retail traders are systematically being used as liquidity by the banks. Understanding liquidity changed everything. For the first time, price action made sense.

Over the next three years, I refined my edge across dozens of currency pairs, commodities, and market conditions. I documented everything. Built systems. Developed a risk framework that has kept me in profit through volatile markets, news events, and sideways conditions.

DecodeFXGroup was born from a simple belief: the knowledge that transforms traders should not be gatekept. Every student who joins gets the same framework that took me years to build, structured, tested, and taught with brutal honesty.

This is not a get-rich-quick academy. This is a professional school for serious traders. If you're ready to treat trading as a business, A group of proffesional traders and certified risk managers at Decode fx academy alongside myself are ready to hold your hands and lead you to profitability.
